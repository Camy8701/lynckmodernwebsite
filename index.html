Our Process

Every step of our process is transparent and collaborative — you’ll always know what we’re doing, why, and how it drives your results.

Step 1 – Discovery & Strategy Alignment

We listen before we launch.

  • Select the service that fits your needs — Google Ads, Website Creation, or the LYNCK Partner.
  • Book a free consultation.
  • We review your business goals, industry, and challenges.
  • We conduct a quick audit (if applicable) and present a tailored action plan.

Step 2 – Building & Optimization Phase

We create. You grow.

  • For Google Ads, we build and optimize campaigns for your goals.
  • For Easy‑Edit Websites, we design, deploy, and hand you an admin dashboard.
  • For Partner Packages, we start your customized training and work on your live campaigns together.
  • You get regular updates, strategy calls (if applicable), and transparent reports.

Step 3 – Handover, Support & Growth

We don’t just deliver — we empower.

  • Once your project or training is complete, we provide detailed documentation and insights.
  • Our support team remains available to help you adapt, update, or scale.
  • For training clients, we include up to 12 months of post‑program mentorship.
